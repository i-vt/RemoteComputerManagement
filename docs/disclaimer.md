## Intended Use

This tool exists for one thing: testing security. Specifically, it is meant for:

- Finetuning personal security products (PSP) and XDR/EDR solutions
- Incident response and penetration testing training
- Threat simulation

That is the complete list. It was built for defenders, trainers, researchers, and students working in environments they control. Anything outside of the uses above is outside the scope of this project, and outside of what I condone.

## Realism

Every feature in this tool is made to be as realistic as possible, and that is deliberate. A security product is only as good as the testing it receives, and a trainee is only as prepared as the scenarios they train against. You cannot meaningfully evaluate an EDR against a threat that behaves like a toy, and you cannot teach incident response with simulations that fall apart the moment something real shows up.

So if something in this tool looks, acts, or feels like the real thing, that is intentional. The realism exists so that detection rules can be measured against actual behavior, response teams can rehearse against something that fights back, and security posture can be assessed honestly instead of optimistically. It is a means to build better defense. It is not, and never will be, an invitation to offend.
